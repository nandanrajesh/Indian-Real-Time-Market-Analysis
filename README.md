# Indian Real-Time Electricity Market Analysis

This repository contains an empirical, multi-temporal analysis of the Indian Real-Time Electricity Market (RTM) using Indian Energy Exchange (IEX) data from April 2022 to June 2025. The study examines market clearing volume (MCV), market clearing price (MCP), final scheduled volume (FSV), and bidding behavior across hourly, seasonal, and yearly time scales to understand market evolution and stability.

## Key Objectives
- Analyze price–volume dynamics in the Indian RTM
- Study intraday, seasonal, and long-term market behavior
- Identify structural shifts in liquidity and market efficiency

## Key Findings
- Steady growth in market clearing volumes indicating improved liquidity
- Declining and stabilizing MCP over time
- Transition from demand-driven to supply-rich market conditions
- Strong inverse relationship between MCV and MCP

## Dataset
- Source: Indian Energy Exchange (IEX)
- Parameters: Purchase bids, sell bids, MCV, FSV, MCP
- Time resolution: 15-minute blocks aggregated to multiple temporal levels

## Tools & Methods
- Data aggregation and statistical analysis
- Multi-temporal analysis (hourly, daily, monthly, seasonal, yearly)
- Visualization of price and volume trends

## Future Scope
